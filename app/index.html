How this sheet works

Leave a score empty for an absent learner (grade 9). Shaded boxes are the 2 best electives that count. Colour outlines (green / yellow / red) are worked out for each learner separately: a learner's strongest third of subjects is green, the weakest third red, and the rest yellow. Choose "Fixed score bands" in the colour box to use 70+ / 50–69 / below 50 instead. Grades: 90+ =1, 80–89 =2, 70–79 =3, 60–69 =4, 55–59 =5, 50–54 =6, 40–49 =7, 35–39 =8, below 35 =9. Default order: lowest aggregate first; equal aggregates are separated by higher raw score. The list re-orders about a second after you stop typing. Learners with a name but no scores stay at the bottom. Data saves automatically on this computer.

The tick includes a subject in the total (untick one that was not tested). Rename, move, remove or add subjects as needed.
How this sheet works

Leave a score empty for an absent learner (counts as 0). TOTAL = sum of the included subjects. POSITION = rank by total (equal totals share a position). Colour outlines are worked out for each learner separately: the strongest third of a learner's subjects is green, the weakest third red, the rest yellow – or choose fixed bands. Decimals such as 20.5 are allowed. The list re-orders about a second after you stop typing. Data saves automatically on this computer.
